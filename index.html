Try step 1 for free

Don't know what your ebook should be about? Ask Claude this.

Copy and paste into Claude
Act as a nonfiction publishing strategist. My background: [YOUR JOBS, SKILLS, HOBBIES, QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK YOU]. Interview me with 8 questions, one at a time. Then propose 12 ebook ideas, each with the specific reader, the problem it solves, and why I am credible to write it. Rank them by how specific the reader is and how urgent the problem is.

That's step 1 of 44. Step 2 shows you how to check real demand on Amazon before you write a single chapter.

I've never published anything. Is it too technical?

No design or coding skills are needed. Claude produces the files; the guide shows you how to check them. Where a platform has a form to fill in, the step shows you what to type.

Yes, with disclosure. Part 1 explains the rules and step 26 shows exactly how to answer KDP's AI-content questions. The guide also teaches you to audit repetition and invented claims so your book is one readers finish.
